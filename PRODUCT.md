## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS pitch site (user choice, 2026-10-06). The client's live site runs on Next.js + Supabase; this build is a redesign concept to win the client, not a production replacement.

## Users

- Tuning enthusiasts and private owners of BMW, MINI, Porsche, Volkswagen and Mercedes, often older models (E30, E36, E46), looking for bodykits, lights, spoilers and trim that fit their exact car.
- Garages and car businesses (B2B accounts, sharper pricing) sourcing parts by article or OEM number.
- Dutch-speaking (Flanders / NL); copy is Dutch.

## Product Purpose

Tiger Auto Parts (by TASER BV, Gent) sells tuning and replacement auto parts online and from their Gent location. Success: a visitor picks their car (brand > model > chassis) or searches an article/OEM number and lands on parts that fit, then buys or contacts them.

## Positioning

Specialist in tuning parts for a focused set of European brands (BMW-heavy: E30/E36/E46/F30), 10.000+ parts in stock, same-day shipping on standard parcels, personal advice from people who know these cars.

## Operating Context

- Vehicle selection by brand: BMW, MINI, PORSCHE, VOLKSWAGEN, MERCEDES.
- Search by artikelnummer, OEM-nummer, or vehicle.
- Categories: Exterieur (Bumpers, Bumpersets, Frontspoilers, Diffusers, Sideskirts, Spoilers, Grillen, Spiegelkappen, Emblemen, Stootlijsten), Verlichting (Koplampen, Achterlichten, Mistlampen, Knipperlichten), Interieur (Pookknoppen, Stuur, Pedalen, Dashboard, Bekleding), Motor / Motorruimte, Koelsysteem, Afdichting.

## Product Principles

1. Fitment first: every path leads to "does this fit my car".
2. Speak like the people at the counter in Gent: direct, knowledgeable, Dutch.
3. Show the cars and the parts, not stock abstractions.
4. Honest delivery and pricing info, visible before checkout.
