# Reference board

Created: 2026-10-06
Mode: Persuade (with an Operate finder)
Design read: commerce homepage for tuning enthusiasts and garages, mode Persuade, precision-catalogue lane (premium automotive restraint)
Dials: variance 5, motion 4, density 5, art direction 6
Sourcing: viewed via Playwright screenshots on 2026-10-06 (akrapovic.com, eventuri.net, maxtondesign.com, kwsuspensions.net); turnermotorsport.com blocked by a bot wall

## Quality bar
- Maxton Design (direct competitor, splitters/bodykits): hero photo with a "Choose your car" finder panel; product cutouts on light tiles; black buttons; upright bold grotesk.
- KW Suspensions: near-black sections, tight upright grotesk headline, single accent used only on the CTA, photo cards.
- Eventuri: total restraint; black, macro product photography, almost no chrome.

## Borrow
- Palette/material: KW, Eventuri -> neutral graphite ground, one accent reserved for action
- Type/hierarchy: Maxton, KW -> upright tight grotesk, no italics, no condensed costume
- Layout/composition: Maxton -> finder as the hero's working element; product cutouts on light plates
- Motion/interaction: Eventuri -> almost none; one hero reveal
- Asset language: client's own photography and product shots, cropped clean (no baked-in price bursts)

## Avoid
- Livery devices: slanted decals, stripes, race-number boards (rejected by the user in v1 as playful, random, cheap)
- Promo artwork with baked-in price bursts as card imagery
- Italic expanded display type

## Direction contract
- Thesis: a parts shop that feels as exact as the parts. Fitment first, calm surface.
- First viewport: left headline + sub + 3-select finder; right the client's garage photo bleeding off the viewport edge.
- System: graphite tokens, Inter Tight / Inter, 4px controls / 8px cards, hairline indexes, orange only for action, price emphasis and one stat.
- Risk: too quiet; brand energy depends on the photography and the orange actions.
